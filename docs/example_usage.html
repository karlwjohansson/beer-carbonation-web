Example of how to use this calculator

There might be multiple usages for this calculator but in this section I will explain my process when bottling from an already carbonated keg. For a good result your beer should have reached CO2 equilibrium at a stable temperature. I usually let my keg sit in my keezer at 1 bar of pressure at 4C for about a week.
Preparations before bottling day:
  • Keg beer and carbonate to a normal level (2 - 2.5 Vol)
  • Clean and sanitize bottles

Using the calculator to make syrup:
  • Note down the beer temperature (I usually use the keezer temperature setpoint)
  • Note down the beer head pressure in bars (I connect my kegland spunding valve to the keg)
  • Enter temperature, pressure, desired Vol and amount of beer that is to be bottled into calculator
  • Follow instructions stating sugar to water ratio and bring to a boil
  • Let the syrup cool (optional)

Preparations at bottling day:
  • Prepare bottling station with bottles
  • and caps
  • Make syrup according to calculator
  • Prepare a small container with some bottling yeast (I usually use the same as was used in the beer)
  • CO2 bottle with connection to keg and counter pressure filler
  • Keg of beer (as close to start of bottling as possble)
